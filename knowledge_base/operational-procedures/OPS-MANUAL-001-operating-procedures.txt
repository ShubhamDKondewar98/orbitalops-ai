SECTION 1 — NORMAL OPERATING RANGES
================================================================================

The following table defines nominal, warning, and critical thresholds for all
monitored AETHER-1 telemetry parameters. All flight operations decisions must
reference these values as baseline.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PARAMETER             | UNIT   | NOMINAL RANGE      | WARNING          | CRITICAL
----------------------|--------|---------------------|------------------|----------
Battery Temperature   | °C     | 10 – 45            | 45 – 60 / <5     | >60 / <0
Battery Charge Level  | %      | 60 – 95            | 40 – 60 / >97    | <40 / >99
Solar Panel Output    | W      | 180 – 220          | 150 – 180        | <150
Fuel Pressure         | kPa    | 220 – 280          | 190 – 220        | <190
Signal Strength       | dBm    | -90 – -60          | -100 – -90       | <-100
Data Transmission Rate| kbps   | 50 – 200           | 20 – 50          | <20
CPU Temperature       | °C     | 20 – 60            | 60 – 70          | >70
Altitude              | km     | 520 – 540          | 510 – 520/>540   | <510/>550
Velocity              | km/s   | 7.60 – 7.80        | 7.50–7.60/>7.80  | <7.50/>7.90
Attitude Stability    | deg    | ±1.0               | ±1.0 – ±3.0      | >±3.0
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

SECTION 2 — TELEMETRY MONITORING PROTOCOLS
================================================================================

2.1 CONTINUOUS MONITORING REQUIREMENTS
---------------------------------------
- All parameters must be sampled at minimum 60-second intervals during normal ops
- During anomaly investigation: increase to 10-second intervals for affected systems
- During maneuver phases: increase to 5-second intervals for attitude and propulsion
- Correlated parameter pairs must be monitored together (see Section 2.2)

2.2 CORRELATED PARAMETER MONITORING
--------------------------------------
The following parameter pairs are known to have operational dependencies.
Anomaly in one should trigger increased monitoring of its correlated parameter:

  Battery Temperature ←→ Solar Panel Output
    (thermal anomalies often correlate with power generation issues)

  CPU Temperature ←→ Attitude Stability
    (CPU throttling directly impacts attitude control loop frequency)

  Signal Strength ←→ Attitude Stability
    (attitude deviation causes antenna misalignment and signal degradation)

  Battery Charge Level ←→ Solar Panel Output
    (declining output causes accelerated battery drain)

  Fuel Pressure ←→ Velocity
    (propulsion anomalies impact orbital velocity over time)

2.3 ALERT ESCALATION PROTOCOL
--------------------------------
Level 1 — INFO    : Log event. No immediate action required. Monitor trend.
Level 2 — WARNING : Notify flight operations team within 5 minutes.
                    Increase monitoring frequency. Begin investigation.
Level 3 — CRITICAL: Notify flight operations team IMMEDIATELY.
                    Initiate emergency response procedure.
                    Consider safe mode entry.

SECTION 3 — STANDARD RESPONSE PROCEDURES
================================================================================

3.1 BATTERY TEMPERATURE ANOMALY RESPONSE
------------------------------------------
Trigger: Battery temperature > 60°C (WARNING) or > 75°C (CRITICAL)

Immediate Actions:
  Step 1 — Verify reading with secondary temperature sensor
  Step 2 — Check solar panel output and attitude stability for correlation
  Step 3 — Reduce non-essential power load by minimum 25%
  Step 4 — If temperature continues rising — initiate thermal load reduction
  Step 5 — If temperature exceeds 80°C — prepare for safe mode entry
  Step 6 — Execute attitude correction to reduce solar exposure if applicable
  Step 7 — Notify systems engineering team for thermal subsystem assessment

Recovery Criteria:
  Battery temperature must remain below 45°C for 30 consecutive minutes
  before declaring nominal status restored.

3.2 SIGNAL LOSS RESPONSE PROCEDURE
-------------------------------------
Trigger: Signal strength < -90dBm (WARNING) or complete loss (CRITICAL)

Immediate Actions:
  Step 1 — Execute automated antenna sweep across S-band frequencies
  Step 2 — Check attitude stability telemetry (last known values)
  Step 3 — If no recovery within 5 minutes — switch to X-band backup
  Step 4 — Attempt uplink on all available frequency bands
  Step 5 — If no contact within 15 minutes — spacecraft enters autonomous
            contingency safe mode (pre-programmed onboard)
  Step 6 — Continue contact attempts every 10 minutes for up to 4 hours
  Step 7 — If contact not restored within 4 hours — escalate to mission director

Recovery Criteria:
  Signal strength must be sustained above -85dBm for 10 consecutive minutes
  before resuming normal command uplink operations.

3.3 FUEL PRESSURE ANOMALY RESPONSE
-------------------------------------
Trigger: Fuel pressure < 220 kPa (WARNING) or < 190 kPa (CRITICAL)

Immediate Actions:
  Step 1 — Monitor pressure trend over 15-minute window
  Step 2 — If declining trend confirmed — suspend all thruster operations
  Step 3 — Check last filter purge date — if >45 days, execute purge (FP-003)
  Step 4 — Execute fuel line purge procedure FP-003
  Step 5 — If pressure does not recover after purge — isolate fuel line segment
  Step 6 — Notify propulsion systems engineer immediately for CRITICAL events

Recovery Criteria:
  Fuel pressure must return above 220 kPa and remain stable for 1 hour.

3.4 SOLAR PANEL OUTPUT DEGRADATION RESPONSE
----------------------------------------------
Trigger: Solar panel output < 180W (WARNING) or < 150W (CRITICAL)

Immediate Actions:
  Step 1 — Check battery charge level for correlated decline
  Step 2 — Adjust spacecraft attitude to optimize solar array angle
  Step 3 — Run panel array diagnostic to identify degraded array
  Step 4 — Implement power load shedding plan (reduce non-critical loads)
  Step 5 — Assess whether output decline is trend-based or sudden
  Step 6 — If sudden drop — check for attitude anomaly or shadow event
  Step 7 — If gradual decline — initiate degradation investigation

Recovery Criteria:
  For sudden drops: output must recover above 180W within 2 hours.
  For gradual degradation: mission continues with adjusted power budget.

3.5 CPU TEMPERATURE ANOMALY RESPONSE
---------------------------------------
Trigger: CPU temperature > 60°C (WARNING) or > 70°C (CRITICAL)

Immediate Actions:
  Step 1 — Identify highest CPU utilization processes
  Step 2 — Suspend non-essential high-computation tasks immediately
  Step 3 — Check attitude stability for correlated degradation
  Step 4 — Reduce scheduled task load — defer imaging and non-critical ops
  Step 5 — If temperature exceeds 75°C — suspend all non-safety tasks
  Step 6 — Ensure attitude control process has guaranteed CPU allocation

Recovery Criteria:
  CPU temperature must remain below 55°C for 20 consecutive minutes.

3.6 ATTITUDE INSTABILITY RESPONSE
------------------------------------
Trigger: Attitude deviation > ±2.0 degrees (WARNING) or > ±3.0 degrees (CRITICAL)

Immediate Actions:
  Step 1 — Check reaction wheel status and momentum levels
  Step 2 — Check signal strength for communication impact assessment
  Step 3 — Check CPU temperature for control loop degradation
  Step 4 — Execute attitude correction maneuver if reaction wheels nominal
  Step 5 — If reaction wheels saturated — activate magnetic torquer desaturation
  Step 6 — Suspend all maneuvers until attitude stabilized
  Step 7 — If attitude uncontrollable — initiate safe mode entry

Recovery Criteria:
  Attitude stability must return to ±1.0 degrees and remain stable for
  15 consecutive minutes before resuming normal operations.

SECTION 4 — SAFE MODE ENTRY CRITERIA
================================================================================

AETHER-1 enters autonomous safe mode when ANY of the following occur:
  - Complete communication loss > 7 minutes
  - Battery charge level < 35%
  - Battery temperature > 85°C
  - CPU temperature > 80°C
  - Attitude deviation > ±5.0 degrees uncontrolled

In safe mode AETHER-1 will:
  - Disable all non-essential systems
  - Orient solar panels toward sun for maximum power generation
  - Reduce CPU load to minimum essential processes only
  - Broadcast beacon signal on all frequency bands every 60 seconds
  - Await ground contact and recovery commands
