INCIDENT SUMMARY
----------------
AETHER-1 experienced a complete communication blackout lasting 47 minutes on
Mission Day 66. All telemetry and command uplink was lost. The spacecraft
autonomously entered safe mode and executed onboard contingency protocols.
Ground team re-established contact after antenna realignment and frequency
switching procedures.

ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS
--------------------
Primary Cause:
  Attitude stability deviation of 3.8 degrees caused the spacecraft's
  directional antenna to misalign with ground station pointing angle.
  The misalignment exceeded the antenna's beamwidth tolerance, resulting
  in complete signal loss.

Contributing Factors:
  1. Attitude control system experienced a reaction wheel momentum
     saturation event, temporarily reducing attitude correction authority.
  2. Ground station did not immediately detect attitude deviation due to
     a 6-minute telemetry polling delay.
  3. Primary frequency band (S-band) had higher susceptibility to
     misalignment losses compared to X-band backup.

ACTIONS TAKEN
-------------
1. Executed antenna sweep protocol across all available frequency bands.
2. Switched communication to X-band backup frequency.
3. Sent attitude correction command upon signal restoration.
4. Desaturated reaction wheels via magnetic torquer activation.
5. Reduced telemetry polling interval from 6 minutes to 90 seconds.

RESOLUTION
----------
Full communication restored after 47 minutes.
Reaction wheel momentum successfully desaturated.
Attitude stability returned to ±0.6 degrees nominal.
No onboard data lost during blackout period.

LESSONS LEARNED
---------------
1. Attitude stability deviation beyond ±2.0 degrees must immediately
   trigger communication risk assessment — runbook updated.
2. Telemetry polling interval of 6 minutes is insufficient for early
   anomaly detection — reduced to 90 seconds operationally.
3. Automatic frequency band switching should be triggered earlier —
   at signal strength -85dBm rather than waiting for complete loss.
4. Reaction wheel momentum saturation should generate its own alert
   independent of attitude stability monitoring.

RECOMMENDATIONS FOR FUTURE MISSIONS
-------------------------------------
1. Implement automatic antenna tracking with real-time attitude feed.
2. Reduce S-band to X-band failover threshold to -85dBm.
3. Add reaction wheel saturation as standalone monitored parameter.
4. Schedule daily reaction wheel desaturation maneuvers proactively.
